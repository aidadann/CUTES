# CUTES Logo Description & Design Specification

## 1. Overview
This document provides a detailed visual and symbolic description of the **CUTES** (Catholic University / UPSI Tertiary Catholic Students Community) logo, associated with **Most Holy Redeemer Church, Tanjung Malim**.

---

## 2. Visual Structure & Layout
* **Badge / Shield Shape:** Diamond (Rhombus) divided into four colored quadrant sections.
* **Background Setting:** Heavenly sky filled with soft white fluffy clouds against blue sky.
* **Central Anchor:** A bold black Latin Cross intersecting the diamond at the exact center.
* **Typographic Split:** The letters **"CU"** appear on the left quadrant and **"ES"** on the right quadrant, separated by the central Cross, spelling **CUTES**.

---

## 3. Key Symbolic Elements

| Element | Description | Symbolism & Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Central Cross** | Solid black Latin Cross positioned at the center axis | **Christ Centered:** Represents salvation, Jesus Christ as the rock and foundation of the student community, and Christian faith at university. |
| **Holy Spirit Flame** | Stylized red/magenta flame enveloping the left arm of the cross | **Pentecost & Spiritual Fire:** Symbolizes the Holy Spirit's guidance, zeal, divine passion, and the call for youth to be the light of the world. |
| **Uplifted Hands** | Pair of black open hands reaching up from the bottom quadrant toward the cross | **Praise, Worship & Service:** Represents youth lifting up their prayers, surrendering to God, fellowship, and serving the church and campus community. |
| **Typography ("CU" & "ES")** | Bold serif white capital letters | **Community Identity:** Highlights the acronym CUTES (Catholic UPSI / University Tertiary Education Students) centered around Christ. |

---

## 4. Color Palette & Liturgical Meanings

* **Bright Yellow / Gold (`#F4EA00` / `#D4AF37`) — Top & Bottom Quadrants:**
  * Symbolizes divine light, wisdom, joy, resurrection, and the papal colors of the Catholic Church.
* **Magenta / Pinkish-Purple (`#D81B60`) — Left Quadrant:**
  * Symbolizes energetic youth vitality, love, and vibrant fellowship.
* **Royal Purple / Violet (`#7B1FA2`) — Right Quadrant:**
  * Symbolizes Christ's sovereignty, spiritual depth, reverence, and liturgical solemnity.
* **Crimson Red (`#E53935`) — Flame:**
  * Symbolizes the Holy Spirit, fire of love, and Pentecost.
* **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) — Lettering:**
  * Symbolizes purity, holiness, and the light of truth.
* **Solid Black (`#1A1A1A`) — Cross & Hands:**
  * Symbolizes steadfastness, humility, and grounding in faith.
