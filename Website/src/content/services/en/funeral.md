**If someone is dying, call the parish first, not last.** A priest will come to
anoint the sick and to pray. Do not wait until the person is unconscious, and do
not worry about the hour.

## When a death occurs

Telephone the parish office. Someone will help you arrange:

1. **Prayers at the wake.** The parish, and usually the family's Basic Ecclesial
   Community, will come to pray the rosary and the prayers for the dead on the
   evenings before the funeral.
2. **The funeral Mass.** Celebrated in the church, at a time agreed with the
   parish priest.
3. **The committal.** At the cemetery or the crematorium, with the final
   commendation.

## What the parish needs to know

The full name of the deceased, the date of death, where they were baptised if
you know it, and where the wake is being held. Nothing else is urgent.

## Afterwards

The parish offers Mass for the dead on request, and remembers all the faithful
departed of the parish in November. Ask the office to have a Mass said on an
anniversary.
