Baptism is the door. It is where a person is joined to Christ and made a member
of the Church, and in this parish it is celebrated with the community present,
not privately.

## Infant baptism

Baptisms are celebrated **TODO (parish office): state which Sunday of the month
and at which Mass.**

Parents and godparents attend one preparation session beforehand. It is not
onerous — an hour, in the parish hall, going through what you will be asked to
promise and why.

## Choosing godparents

The Church asks for at least one godparent who is a baptised, confirmed and
practising Catholic, aged sixteen or over, and who is not the child's parent. A
second sponsor may be a baptised Christian of another denomination, who stands
as a Christian witness.

Choose someone who will actually be around. The role is not honorary.

## Adults and older children

An adult asking for baptism is welcomed through [RCIA](../rcia). A child over
the age of seven follows a shorter, adapted version of the same journey.

## Emergency baptism

If a child is in danger of death, any person may baptise by pouring water over
the head and saying: *"I baptise you in the name of the Father, and of the Son,
and of the Holy Spirit."* Then tell the parish, so it can be recorded.
