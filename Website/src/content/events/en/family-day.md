Family Day closes the CUTES year. It is the day the graduating seniors hand the
community over, and the day the alumni turn up to see what has become of it.

## The shape of the day

Morning games — the kind that require no skill and produce a great deal of
shouting. Lunch. A slot in the afternoon where the outgoing committee says what
it has to say and the incoming committee is introduced. Mass in the evening, and
food afterwards that goes on far too long.

## Alumni

If you were part of CUTES and you are anywhere near Tanjung Malim in July, come.
You do not need an invitation and nobody will ask you to give a speech unless
you want to.

## Bring

Clothes you can run in, a change for the afternoon, and something for the potluck
if the recreation ministry has put you on the list.
