A sacred display featuring first, second, and third class relics of holy saints
for veneration, prayer, and reflection.

## What to expect

The relics are placed in the church for the day. Come, pray before them, and
stay as long as you wish. Members of the community will be on hand to explain
what each relic is and whose life it belongs to.
