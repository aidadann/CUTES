A day for the music and choir ministries to work on what they do every week:
the psalm, the hymns, the parts that never quite line up, and the sound of the
room they sing in.

Open to anyone who sings or plays, and to anyone thinking about it. You do not
need to read music and you do not need to be in a ministry already.
