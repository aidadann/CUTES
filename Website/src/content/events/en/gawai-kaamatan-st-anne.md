The diocesan Gawai and Kaamatan celebration, held at the Minor Basilica of St.
Anne in Bukit Mertajam, draws East Malaysian Catholics from every parish in the
diocese for three days.

CUTES travels up as a group. It is the one weekend in the year when our Sabahan
and Sarawakian students are in a room with several hundred people who keep the
same feast in the same way.
