The rosary is a way of walking through the life of Christ with his mother. It
needs no book and no priest, and it can be prayed anywhere — on a bus, in a
hospital corridor, or in the church before Mass.

## How to pray it

1. On the crucifix, make the Sign of the Cross and say the **Apostles' Creed**.
2. On the first bead, an **Our Father**.
3. On the next three beads, three **Hail Marys** — for faith, hope and love.
4. A **Glory Be**.
5. Announce the **first mystery**, then say an **Our Father**.
6. On each of the ten beads, a **Hail Mary**, thinking about the mystery.
7. A **Glory Be**, and the **Fatima Prayer**.
8. Repeat from step 5 for the remaining four mysteries.
9. Close with the **Hail Holy Queen**.

One set of ten beads is called a decade. Five decades make a rosary.

## The Mysteries

### Joyful — Monday and Saturday

1. The Annunciation
2. The Visitation
3. The Nativity
4. The Presentation in the Temple
5. The Finding of Jesus in the Temple

### Sorrowful — Tuesday and Friday

1. The Agony in the Garden
2. The Scourging at the Pillar
3. The Crowning with Thorns
4. The Carrying of the Cross
5. The Crucifixion

### Glorious — Wednesday and Sunday

1. The Resurrection
2. The Ascension
3. The Descent of the Holy Spirit
4. The Assumption of Mary
5. The Coronation of Mary

### Luminous — Thursday

1. The Baptism in the Jordan
2. The Wedding at Cana
3. The Proclamation of the Kingdom
4. The Transfiguration
5. The Institution of the Eucharist

## The Fatima Prayer

O my Jesus, forgive us our sins, save us from the fires of hell,
and lead all souls to heaven, especially those in most need of thy mercy. Amen.

## Hail Holy Queen

Hail, holy Queen, Mother of mercy, our life, our sweetness and our hope.
To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve;
to thee do we send up our sighs, mourning and weeping in this valley of tears.
Turn then, most gracious advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us,
and after this our exile show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus.
O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary.

> Pray for us, O holy Mother of God.
> That we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ. Amen.

## In the parish

The rosary is prayed before the Saturday evening Mass, and every evening during
October and May. During Lent it is prayed alongside the Stations of the Cross.
