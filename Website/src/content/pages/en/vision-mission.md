## Our Vision

A parish family in Tanjung Malim where every person — resident, migrant worker
and student far from home — finds a place at the table, is known by name, and is
sent out to serve.

## Our Mission

We gather around the Eucharist, form disciples of every age, and serve the town
we live in.

- **Worship.** To celebrate the sacraments faithfully and beautifully, in the
  languages our people pray in.
- **Formation.** To teach the faith plainly, to children, to adults preparing
  for baptism, and to the university students who pass through for a few years.
- **Community.** To keep the Basic Ecclesial Communities alive so that no one
  in this parish is anonymous.
- **Service.** To be of practical use to Tanjung Malim, especially to those the
  town overlooks.
