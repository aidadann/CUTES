A **Basic Ecclesial Community** — BEC, or *Komuniti Kristian Asas* — is a small
group of Catholic households who live near one another and meet regularly to
pray, read the Sunday gospel together, and look after each other.

It is the ordinary structure of parish life in Malaysia, and it is the answer to
a real problem: a parish is too big for anyone to be known in. A BEC is about a
dozen families. In a BEC, an absence is noticed.

## What a meeting looks like

A meeting is usually an hour, in someone's living room, once or twice a month.
There is a hymn, a passage from the coming Sunday's gospel, a conversation about
what it means for this week, and prayers for whatever the group is carrying —
an illness, an exam, a job. Then supper, which takes longer than the meeting.

## Students and BECs

Students are welcome in the BEC of whichever area they live in during term, and
CUTES itself functions as a BEC for those on campus. If you would like to join
one, ask the parish office or any CUTES committee member.
