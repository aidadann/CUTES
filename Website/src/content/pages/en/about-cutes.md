CUTES stands for **Catholic UPSI Tertiary Education Students**. We are the
Catholic students of Universiti Pendidikan Sultan Idris, and we belong to the
parish of Most Holy Redeemer, Tanjung Malim.

Most of us arrive here at eighteen, from Sabah, Sarawak, Perak and everywhere
else, and for many of us it is the first time we have had to decide for
ourselves whether to go to Mass. CUTES exists so that the decision is not made
alone.

## What we do

We serve the Sunday evening Mass — reading, singing, playing, setting up and
packing down. We eat together afterwards. We run retreats, a Taize evening once
a month, a games day, and the Gawai and Kaamatan celebrations that keep the East
Malaysian students connected to home.

## What the logo means

The CUTES emblem is a diamond divided into four quadrants, with a black Latin
cross at the centre and the letters **CU** and **ES** on either side of it.

- The **cross** at the centre: Christ is the foundation, including at university.
- The **flame** on the left arm: the Holy Spirit, Pentecost, and the call to
  young people to be light.
- The **uplifted hands** below: praise, surrender, and service.
- The **gold, magenta and purple** quadrants: divine light, the vitality of
  youth, and the reverence owed to Christ the King.

## Joining

There is nothing to sign. Come to the Sunday evening Mass at 6:00 pm, stay for
the food afterwards, and someone will find you. If you would rather write first,
the coordinator's address is on the [contact page](../contact).
