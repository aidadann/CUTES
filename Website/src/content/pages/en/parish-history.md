## Beginnings

Catholics have lived and worked around Tanjung Malim since the estates and the
railway brought families to this stretch of the Perak–Selangor border. For years
there was no church here. A priest travelled up from [nearest parish] and Mass
was said in a house, then in a rented shophouse.

## The students

The opening of Universiti Pendidikan Sultan Idris changed the shape of the
congregation. Every year a few hundred Catholic students arrive, many of them
from Sabah and Sarawak, most of them away from home for the first time. CUTES
grew out of that — a community within the parish, run by students, for students.

## Today

The parish serves the town, the estates around it, and the university. On a
Sunday evening the church is full of people in their twenties, and on a Sunday
morning it is full of the families who built the place. Both are the parish.
