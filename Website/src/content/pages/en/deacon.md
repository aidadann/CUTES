## Deacon [Name]

Deacon [Name] was ordained to the diaconate in [year]. He assists at the altar,
proclaims the Gospel, preaches, and prepares couples for marriage and parents
for the baptism of their children.

A deacon is ordained for service — of the liturgy, of the word, and of charity.
In this parish that last part looks like hospital visits, the food collection,
and the quiet work of knowing who is missing on a Sunday.
