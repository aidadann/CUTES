# FIXES V1 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Apply the committee's FIXES V1 revision to the parish website — new branding, hero, weekend Mass block, pastoral council section, event list, footer/contact details, and rewritten CUTES pages.

**Architecture:** This is an Astro 5 static site with no database and no CMS. Facts live in `src/data/site.ts`, interface strings in `src/i18n/ui.ts` (English and Bahasa Malaysia side by side), the site map in `src/i18n/nav.ts`, and page prose in Markdown/YAML under `src/content/`. Almost every change below is an edit to one of those four places; the `.astro` templates only change where the *shape* of a section changes (two weekend days instead of one Sunday, three council profiles instead of one bulletin teaser, two community links instead of one).

**Tech Stack:** Astro 5, Tailwind CSS 4, TypeScript, Vitest, Astro content collections with Zod schemas.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-21-fixes-v1.md` — read it alongside this plan. Its "Open questions resolved by the implementer" section explains every place the plan's wording differs from the raw PDF transcription.

## Global Constraints

- **Working directory for every command is `Website/`.** Paths in this plan are written relative to `Website/` unless they start with `Requirement/` or `docs/`.
- **Never hand-write an internal `href` with a leading slash.** Every internal link goes through `localePath(lang, path)` and every `public/` asset through `assetPath(path)` (both from `src/i18n/utils.ts`). The site deploys both at `/` and at `/CUTES/`; a hand-written path silently 404s on the second.
- **Every UI string is added to both `ui.en` and `ui.ms` in `src/i18n/ui.ts`.** A missing `ms` key falls back to English silently, which hides the omission.
- **Copy is verbatim from the spec.** Do not "improve" the committee's wording, punctuation or capitalisation. Headlines that the spec gives in sentence case are rendered uppercase by CSS/`uppercase` classes already present — keep the existing `uppercase` Tailwind classes and store the string as the spec writes it, in the case the surrounding strings already use (existing headline keys are stored UPPERCASE; keep that convention).
- **Exact values, copied verbatim:**
  - Church name (header/brand): `Church Of The Most Holy Redeemer`
  - Church name (hero tag, page titles, SEO): `Most Holy Redeemer Church`
  - Diocese: `Archdiocese of Penang`
  - Postal address: `Catholic Church Of The Most Holy Redeemer (1960), Jalan Rest House, 35900 Tanjong Malim, Perak`
  - Email: `cutesfamilyy@gmail.com`
  - Fr Vincent Paul — Parish Priest — `019-4930768`
  - Deacon Isaac Alfred — Parish Deacon — `012-6946343`
  - Ryon Joshua Harry — CUTES Koordinator — `017-8970158`
  - Instagram `cutes_family`, TikTok `cutesfamily_`, Facebook `Catholic Undergraduate Teachers' Society (CUTES) UPSI`
  - CUTES expands to `Catholic Undergraduate Teachers' Society`
- **Apostrophes:** the codebase uses the typographic apostrophe (`’`) in prose strings. `Teachers’ Society` in prose; a straight `'` only inside code identifiers.
- **Run `npm run build` before every commit.** It runs `astro check` first, which type-checks every `.astro` file and validates every content collection against its Zod schema. A red build is the only automated gate most of these tasks have.
- **Commit after every task**, with the `fix:` or `feat:` prefix shown in the task.

```markdown
title: About CUTES
summary: Catholic Undergraduate Teachers’ Society — the Catholic UPSI student community at Most Holy Redeemer Church.
section: cutes
order: 1
---

CUTES stands for **Catholic Undergraduate Teachers’ Society**. As Catholic
students attending Universiti Pendidikan Sultan Idris (UPSI), we form a vibrant
student community rooted in the parish of Most Holy Redeemer, Tanjung Malim.

Our members come together from all over Malaysia including Sabah, Sarawak, and
Peninsular Malaysia. Pursuing our studies across Diploma, Degree, Master’s, and
PhD programs, we find ourselves at a crucial transition where practicing our
faith becomes a personal choice. CUTES offers a welcoming spiritual and social
brotherhood and sisterhood so that no student has to navigate their university
years or faith journey alone.

## What we do

We serve the Sunset Mass, taking care of the liturgy, readings, choir, music,
and venue setup. After Mass, we always gather for meals to catch up and bond.

Outside the church, CUTES stays active through sports days, student outreach
programs, and community service in nearby villages. We also host the CUTES
Retreat, CUTES Family Day, and celebrate Gawai and Kaamatan to keep our East
Malaysian traditions strong on campus.

## What the logo means

- **The cross** — the sign of our salvation as Catholic students and as God's people.
- **The hands** — our service as students and as teachers.
- **The flame** — the burning red fire that carries our zeal to keep proclaiming the Gospel.
- **The colours, yellow, pink and purple** — the colours of festivity in the Liturgy.

## Joining CUTES

There are no registration forms to sign, once you step into UPSI, you are
officially part of the CUTES family! Just join us for Sunset Mass at 7:30 pm and
stay for food afterwards. If you'd like to get involved in serving or need any
support, our doors are always open, feel free to approach any member after Mass
or reach out directly to our coordinator via the [contact page](../contact).
```
