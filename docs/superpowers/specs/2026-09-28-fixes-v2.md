## Open questions for the committee

1. **Ministry of Leaders office titles.** The site renders Coordinator, Vice
   Coordinator I/II, Treasurer, Vice Treasurer, Secretary and Vice Secretary.
   V2 lists exactly seven names in that order, and FIXES V1 already confirms
   Ryon Joshua Harry as CUTES Coordinator, so the plan maps the seven names to
   the seven offices positionally. The committee must confirm.
2. **Ministry leaders.** V2 names no leader or assistant for the other seven
   ministries, so every member in them is published without a title until the
   committee supplies one.
3. **Christmas Caroling date.** Left as "to be confirmed" on the site.
4. **Workshop and caroling venues.** Not supplied; left as a build-visible TODO.
5. **"Diocese of Penang" vs "Archdiocese of Penang".** V2's event copy says
   "Diocese of Penang"; `src/data/site.ts` says "Archdiocese of Penang" on the
   committee's own FIXES V1 instruction. The committee's V2 wording is
   reproduced verbatim inside the event text and `site.diocese` is left alone.
   The committee should say which is right.
